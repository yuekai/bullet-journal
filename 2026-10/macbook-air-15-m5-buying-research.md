Research for buying a 15-inch MacBook Air with at least 24GB RAM and 1TB SSD, new or refurbished from a reputable dealer with a warranty. The buyer has an Apple education discount. Prices checked 2026-10-17; no purchase made yet.

## Market context

- The current generation is the M5 (launched early 2026). Apple raised MacBook Air prices mid-2026; the 15-inch M5 24GB/1TB now lists at $1,999 (was $1,799 before the hike).
- Spring 2026 lows ($1,499–1,549 for this config) predate the hike and are unlikely to return soon; October 2026 discounts are the best since the hike. Black Friday might shave a little more, but waiting is a gamble.

## Best options for the 15-inch M5 24GB/1TB

- **Best Buy open box** — roughly $1,573–1,755 depending on condition grade and store; a Fair-grade Midnight unit was live at $1,680.99 with several available. Geek Squad-verified, Best Buy return window, and nearly all of Apple's 1-year warranty remains on these spring-2026 machines (verify the serial at Apple's coverage checker). Education/student discounts do not apply to open box, but it wins anyway.
- **Amazon, new** — Midnight at $1,799 (10% off, ASIN B0GR1RWSMF) and Starlight at $1,849 (B0GR19L8YJ), live 2026-10-17; B&H also at $1,849. Sealed, full 1-year Apple warranty, 30-day returns.
- **Apple Education Store** — $1,899: only $100 off this config, so street prices beat it. Education pricing does not stack with Apple Certified Refurbished or open box, and the 2026 back-to-school gift-card promo ended in late September. AppleCare+ for the 15-inch Air runs about $239 education vs $245 retail (3-year) after Apple's July 2026 increase — a wash; cheaper to buy the hardware elsewhere and add AppleCare+ if wanted.
- **Budget refurb alternatives** (1-year warranty, 30-day returns): Back Market certified-refurbished M4 15-inch 24GB/1TB about $1,627; M2 15-inch 24GB/1TB about $1,132 (24GB/2TB about $1,198). The M4 is dominated by the Best Buy open-box M5 at similar money; the M2 only makes sense if $600+ of savings matters more than speed.
- **Apple Certified Refurbished** had nothing qualifying in stock: the $1,529 15-inch is 16GB RAM, and only 4TB oddballs remain (13-inch 24GB/4TB $2,799; 15-inch 32GB/4TB $3,139). A 24GB/1TB config would list around $1,699 when restocked, so it is worth rechecking before buying.
- 13-inch models were ruled out by the buyer early on; all prices above are 15-inch.
